# Beginner 3-Day Strength Plan: Day by Day

**Equipment:** dumbbells, resistance band, bench (or sturdy chair), foam roller.

## How to read the numbers

- A **rep** is one full movement, like squatting down and standing back up once.
- A **set** is a group of reps done in a row without stopping.
- **3 sets of 10 reps** means: do the move 10 times, rest, do it 10 more times, rest, then do it 10 more times. That's 30 reps in total.
- **"8–10 reps"** means aim for anywhere from 8 to 10. Start at 8 and work up to 10 over the weeks.
- **"per side"** means do all the reps on one side, then all the reps on the other side. That counts as one set.
- **Rest 60–90 seconds** between sets, which is enough time to catch your breath.
- **Choosing a weight:** pick one where the last 2 or 3 reps of a set feel hard but you could still do 2 or 3 more with good form.

## Warm-up (do this before every workout, 6–8 minutes)

1. **March in place** briskly for 3 minutes.
2. **[Foam roll your quads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mrHET_4nL7U)** (front of your thighs) for 30 seconds.
3. **[Foam roll your upper back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cqd2USvPuCA)** for 30 seconds.
4. **[Bodyweight squats](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dmgJ1rmoR4U)**: 10 reps, no weights.
5. **[Band pull-aparts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20rhTxoJVuw)**: 15 reps.
6. **[Glute bridges](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nuapk_-Q2BI)**: 10 reps.

---

## Workout A (about 35–45 minutes)

Do the warm-up first. Then, for each exercise, finish all its sets before moving to the next one.

1. **[Goblet squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MeIiIdhvXT4)**: 3 sets of 8–10 reps.
   Hold one dumbbell at your chest, squat down, stand back up.
2. **[Dumbbell floor press](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUGDRwge4F8)** (or on a bench): 3 sets of 8–10 reps.
   Lie on your back and press the dumbbells up from your chest.
3. **[One-arm dumbbell row](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pYcpY20QaE8)**: 3 sets of 10 reps per side.
   One hand and knee on the bench, pull the dumbbell up toward your hip.
4. **[Glute bridge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nuapk_-Q2BI)**: 2 sets of 12 reps.
   Lie on your back, knees bent, lift your hips and squeeze.
5. **[Dead bug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bxn9FBrt4-A)**: 2 sets of 8 reps per side.
   Lie on your back, slowly lower the opposite arm and leg while keeping your lower back on the floor.
6. **Cool down** with the foam roller routine below.

---

## Workout B (about 35–45 minutes)

Do the warm-up first. Then, for each exercise, finish all its sets before moving to the next one.

1. **[Dumbbell Romanian deadlift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQKfr1YDhEk)**: 3 sets of 10 reps.
   Knees slightly bent, push your hips back and lower the dumbbells along your legs, then stand up.
2. **[Split squat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vTlcCN-Kbr4)**: 2 sets of 8 reps per leg.
   One foot forward, one back, lower your back knee toward the floor.
3. **[Dumbbell overhead press](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qu4-bpZrtI)**: 3 sets of 8 reps.
   Press the dumbbells from your shoulders up over your head.
4. **[Band row](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ysAjxPSFC7M)**: 3 sets of 12 reps.
   Anchor the band to a door or post and pull it toward your stomach.
5. **[Plank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQu26ABuVS0)**: 3 holds of 20–30 seconds.
   Forearms and toes on the floor, body in a straight line. Drop to your knees if needed.
6. **Cool down** with the foam roller routine below.

## Foam roller cool-down (after every workout, 5–8 minutes)

Roll slowly, spending 30–60 seconds on each area. Pause on tight spots, but it should never be sharp pain.

1. **Quads** (front of thighs)
2. **Glutes** (sit on the roller, cross one ankle over the other knee)
3. **Calves**
4. **Upper back**

The [leg rolling video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mrHET_4nL7U) covers quads, glutes and calves; the [upper back video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cqd2USvPuCA) covers the last one. Skip your lower back and don't roll directly over joints.

---

## Rest days (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday)

1. Take a 20–30 minute walk.
2. If you're sore, do the foam roller cool-down above.

## Getting stronger over time

When you can do the top number of reps on every set of an exercise (for example, 3 sets of 10 on the goblet squat), use a slightly heavier weight the next time you do it.
