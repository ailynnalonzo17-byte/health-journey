# Beginner 3-Day Strength Plan

**Equipment:** dumbbells, resistance band, bench (or sturdy chair), foam roller.

## Schedule

Train Monday, Wednesday and Friday, alternating workouts:

- Week 1: A, B, A
- Week 2: B, A, B

Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Pick weights that leave 2–3 reps in the tank.

## Warm-up (6–8 min, every session)

1. Brisk march in place, 3 min
2. Foam roll quads and upper back, 30 s each
3. Bodyweight squats × 10
4. Band pull-aparts × 15
5. Glute bridges × 10

## Main lifts

| Day A | Day B |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat 3 × 8–10 | Dumbbell Romanian deadlift 3 × 10 |
| Dumbbell bench or floor press 3 × 8–10 | Split squat 2 × 8 per leg |
| One-arm dumbbell row 3 × 10 per side | Dumbbell overhead press 3 × 8 |
| Glute bridge 2 × 12 | Band row or pulldown 3 × 12 |
| Dead bug 2 × 8 per side | Plank 3 × 20–30 s |

## Progression

Once you hit the top of the rep range on every set of an exercise, add a small amount of weight the next session.

## Foam roller recovery (5–8 min after each session)

Roll slowly for 30–60 s on each area:

- Quads
- Glutes
- Calves
- Upper back

Skip the lower back and joints. On rest days, a 20–30 min walk plus a quick roll helps with soreness.
