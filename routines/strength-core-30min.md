# 30-Minute Strength & Core Routine

**Equipment:** A pair of dumbbells (or water jugs or a loaded backpack) and a mat. You can do it all with body weight if you need to.
**Frequency:** 2–3 times a week, with at least one rest day in between.

---

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Do each move for 45 seconds, then rest 15 seconds.

1. March in place or do jumping jacks
2. Arm circles, forward and back
3. Bodyweight squats, slow and controlled
4. Hip hinges (good mornings) with your hands on your hips
5. Cat-cow, then 2–3 inchworms

---

## 2. Strength Circuit (12 min)
Do **3 rounds**: 40 seconds of work, then 20 seconds of rest per move. Rest 30–60 seconds between rounds.

| # | Exercise | Easier option | Harder option |
|---|----------|---------------|---------------|
| 1 | Goblet squat | Squat to a chair | Pause 2 seconds at the bottom |
| 2 | Push-ups | On your knees or hands on a bench | Feet raised |
| 3 | Romanian deadlift (dumbbells) | Body weight, hands on thighs | Single-leg |
| 4 | Bent-over dumbbell row | One arm, other hand on a chair | 3-second lowering |

---

## 3. Core Circuit (10 min)
Do **2 rounds**: 40 seconds of work, then 20 seconds of rest per move.

| # | Exercise | Cue |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | Forearm plank | Squeeze your glutes and keep your body in a straight line |
| 2 | Dead bug | Keep your lower back pressed into the floor and move slowly |
| 3 | Glute bridge | Drive through your heels and pause at the top |
| 4 | Side plank (20 seconds per side) | Stack your hips; drop your bottom knee to make it easier |
| 5 | Bird dog | Reach long and keep your hips level |

---

## 4. Cool-down (3 min)
Hold each stretch for about 30 seconds.

- Child's pose
- Figure-4 glute stretch (each side)
- Standing hamstring stretch
- Chest-opener doorway stretch
- 5 slow, deep breaths

---

## Tips
- **Form first:** Slow, controlled reps beat fast, sloppy ones.
- **Progress:** When the last few reps feel easy, add weight, slow down the tempo, or switch to the harder option.
- **Breathe:** Breathe out on the effort, such as pushing up or standing up.
- **Listen to your body:** Stop if you feel sharp pain. If you have injuries or health conditions, check with a healthcare provider first.
