# 20-minute home strength plan

Started 2026-09-25. 2 or 3 times a week, on days apart (for example Mon, Wed, Fri). Kickboxing can be on the same day or the days between.

Equipment: a sturdy chair, a wall or kitchen counter, and 2 dumbbells or filled water jugs (a 1 gallon jug weighs about 8 lb).

## Warm-up (2 min)
March in place 1 min, arm circles 30 s, hip circles 30 s.

## Circuit (15 min)
Each move 40 s work, 20 s rest. Do all 5, then repeat. 3 rounds.

| # | Move | How | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chair squat | Sit back to touch the chair, stand up tall. Push through the heels. | Use your hands on your thighs | Hold weights; don't touch the chair |
| 2 | Counter push-up | Hands on counter edge, body straight, lower your chest to it. | Hands on a wall | Hands on a low chair seat |
| 3 | Glute bridge | Lie on your back, knees bent, squeeze your glutes and lift your hips. | Smaller lift | Weight on hips, or one leg |
| 4 | Bent-over row | Hinge forward with a flat back, pull the weights to your ribs. | Lighter weight | Heavier weight, 2 s lowering |
| 5 | Dead bug | On your back, arms up, knees at 90°. Slowly lower the opposite arm and leg, keep your lower back pressed down. | Move only your legs | Hold a weight in your hands |

## Cool-down (3 min)
Hamstring stretch, chest stretch at a doorway, child's pose. 30 to 45 s each.

## Progress
- Weeks 1 and 2: learn the moves; stop each set with 2 to 3 good reps still in the tank.
- Weeks 3 and 4: use the harder version or heavier weight on any move that feels easy.
- After week 4: 45 s work, 15 s rest.

## Safety
- No crunches or sit-ups for now. Dead bugs work the deep core without pushing the belly out (safer if there is any muscle separation).
- Sharp or joint pain means stop that move. Muscle burn is fine.
- Log it as "20 min strength" (about 100 kcal at her weight).
