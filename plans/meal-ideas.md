# Meal ideas (labs-friendly)

Limit 1,799 kcal, protein 137 g. Focus: lean protein, fish, vegetables, 1 cup rice max per meal, less fatty pork, fried food and sweets.

## Breakfast (250 to 400)
| Meal | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Premier shake + banana | 265 | 31 g |
| 2 eggs + 2 egg whites scrambled with tomato and onion, 1 slice whole wheat toast | ~300 | 25 g |
| Oatmeal (40 g dry) made with water, stir in a Premier shake, berries | ~330 | 36 g |
| Light + Fit Greek yogurt, ½ cup berries, 2 tbsp oats | ~190 | 14 g |

## Lunch (450 to 550)
| Meal | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Grilled or steamed bangus 150 g, 1 cup brown rice, steamed bok choy or broccoli | ~540 | 45 g |
| Chicken adobo (skinless breast 150 g, little oil), 1 cup brown rice, green beans | ~520 | 50 g |
| Sinigang na hipon or isda with lots of vegetables, ½ to 1 cup rice | ~400 | 35 g |
| Chicken tinola (breast, sayote or papaya, malunggay), ½ cup rice | ~400 | 42 g |

## Dinner (400 to 550)
| Meal | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| Salmon 130 g, 100 g sweet potato, broccoli | ~400 | 32 g |
| Grilled pompano, tomato and onion salad, 1 cup brown rice | ~550 | 38 g |
| Shrimp or chicken stir-fry with mixed vegetables (pinakbet style, no pork, light bagoong), 1 cup rice | ~450 | 35 g |
| Pesto chicken breast 150 g, big salad, 20 g parmesan | ~400 | 52 g |

## Snacks (100 to 200)
Premier shake (160, 30 g) · Greek yogurt (80, 12 g) · 2 hard-boiled eggs (140, 12 g) · 1 cup edamame (190, 17 g) · apple or orange · cucumber or jicama

## Sample day (~1,550 kcal, ~165 g protein)
Premier + banana → bangus, rice, vegetables → Greek yogurt → chicken tinola with ½ cup rice → Premier shake.

## Eating out
- Jollibee: 1 Chickenjoy (skin off) with rice OR spaghetti, not both. Skip the pie.
- McDonald's: Egg McMuffin (~310) instead of Big Breakfast (~760).
- Subway: 6-inch turkey or chicken on wheat, lots of vegetables, no mayo.
- Tacos: 2 to 3 chicken or carne asada on corn tortillas, no cheese or sour cream.
- Freddy's: single steakburger, skip fries or share, no custard.

## Easy swaps
Longganisa 4 pcs → 2 pcs plus an egg · Ribs or sisig → grilled chicken or fish · Banana bread → apple · Parmesan 65 g → 20 g · 1½ cups rice → 1 cup
