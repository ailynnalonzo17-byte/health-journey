# Weekly routine for belly fat (made 2026-11-16)

Belly fat cannot be spot-burned; it comes off with overall fat loss. Cardio + strength + core + fewer sweets.

## Weekly plan
| Day | Workout |
|---|---|
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Brisk walk or walking pad 4 mph, 30 min + strength 20 to 30 min (see `home-strength-20min.md`, kettlebell, Meredith / Strength and Svelte) + core |
| Tue, Thu | Intervals 20 to 30 min: walking pad 1 min fast or incline, 2 min normal; or trampoline bursts |
| Sat | Long walk, mall walk, pickleball or dancing |
| Sun | Rest or easy walk |

## Core (3 to 4 times a week)
| Move | Sets x reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plank | 3 x 20 to 60 s | She did 3 x 1 min on 11/16. Next: 1 min 15 s or side planks |
| Dead bug | 2 x 10 each side | Lower back stays on the floor; 2 lb dumbbells OK |
| Bird dog | 2 x 10 each side | Slow, hips level |
| Glute bridge | 2 x 15 | Squeeze at the top |

## Her kettlebell / dumbbell moves (6 kg kettlebell, 2 lb dumbbells)
- Kettlebell around the waist: 100 passes
- Standing leg raise with kettlebell hold: 50 each leg
- Squats with 2 lb dumbbells: 50

## Habits
- Less sugar and sweet drinks (boba, brownie custard, pastries)
- Sleep 7 to 8 h; manage stress
- Protein 137 g a day
- Measure waist every 2 weeks, morning, at the belly button. 41 in (Sep 23) -> 40 in (Nov 16). Next check about Nov 30.
