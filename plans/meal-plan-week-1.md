# 1-week meal plan (week of 9 Oct 2026)

Targets: under 1,799 kcal, about 137 g protein. Built for the Oct labs: lean protein and fish, vegetables, 1 cup rice max, little fatty pork, one planned treat.
Swap any day for another. Weigh rice cooked (1 cup ≈ 195 g). Recipes: `plans/recipes.md`.

## Grocery list
- Protein: Premier shakes (about 10), eggs (2 dozen), chicken breast (about 3 lb), bangus, salmon, tilapia, pompano, shrimp (about 1 lb), lean sirloin (about ½ lb), canned tuna in water, Light + Fit Greek yogurt (5), frozen edamame
- Carbs: brown rice, rolled oats, whole wheat bread, whole wheat tortillas, corn tortillas, sweet potatoes
- Vegetables: bok choy, broccoli, green beans, sayote or green papaya, malunggay, kangkong, radish and okra (sinigang), eggplant, squash, ampalaya, tomatoes, onions, garlic, ginger, salad greens, cucumber
- Fruit: bananas, apples, oranges, berries
- Other: tamarind sinigang mix, low-sodium soy sauce, vinegar, pesto (small jar), salsa, light mayo, vinaigrette

## Prep tips
- Sunday: cook a pot of brown rice and portion into 1-cup containers; bake 2 lb of chicken breast.
- Grill or bake fish instead of frying.
- Fill half the plate with vegetables first.
- Water: 64 oz a day.
