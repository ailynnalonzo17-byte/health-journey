# Recipes for the 1-week meal plan

Each recipe is 1 serving unless noted. Calories are for the recipe as written. Use a nonstick pan or cooking spray so you need very little oil. Rice is measured cooked (1 cup ≈ 195 g).

---

## Breakfasts

### Oatmeal protein bowl (Tue, Sun) · ~330 kcal · 36 g protein
- 40 g rolled oats (½ cup dry)
- ¾ cup water
- 1 Premier Protein shake (vanilla or chocolate)
- ¼ cup berries

1. Microwave the oats and water for 2 minutes. Stir.
2. Pour in about half the Premier shake and stir until creamy. Drink the rest, or add it all.
3. Top with berries.

### Veggie egg scramble with toast (Wed, Sat) · ~300 kcal · 25 g protein
- 2 eggs + 2 egg whites
- ½ tomato, chopped; ¼ onion, chopped
- Pinch of salt and pepper; cooking spray
- 1 slice whole wheat toast

1. Spray a nonstick pan. Cook onion 2 minutes, add tomato for 1 minute.
2. Beat the eggs and whites, pour in, and stir gently until just set.
3. Serve with the toast.

### Yogurt bowl with eggs (Fri) · ~290 kcal · 26 g protein
- 1 Light + Fit Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup berries, 2 tbsp rolled oats
- 2 hard-boiled eggs (boil 10 minutes, cool in cold water)

Mix the yogurt, berries and oats. Eat the eggs on the side.

No need to cook the oats: rolled oats are steamed at the factory and safe to eat raw. For softer oats, let the bowl sit 10 minutes, or mix it the night before and keep it in the fridge (overnight oats). For warm oats, microwave them with a splash of water for 30 seconds first. Same calories either way.

## Fish

### Grilled bangus (Mon lunch) · ~285 kcal fish only · 39 g protein
- 150 g boneless bangus (daing-style butterflied or belly-less fillet)
- 1 tbsp calamansi or lemon juice, 1 clove garlic (minced), pepper, pinch of salt

1. Rub the fish with calamansi, garlic, pepper and salt. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Grill, air-fry (200 °C / 400 °F, 12 to 15 min) or bake skin side up until crisp.
3. Serve with 1 cup brown rice and steamed bok choy (3 to 4 min in a covered pan with a splash of water). Plate total ~513 kcal.

### Sheet-pan salmon with sweet potato and broccoli (Mon dinner) · ~410 kcal · 35 g protein
- 130 g salmon fillet
- 100 g sweet potato, cubed
- 1½ cups broccoli florets
- 1 tsp olive oil, garlic powder, salt, pepper, lemon

1. Heat oven to 200 °C / 400 °F. Toss sweet potato with half the oil; roast 15 minutes.
2. Add broccoli and salmon to the pan, brush with remaining oil, season.
3. Roast 12 to 15 minutes more. Squeeze lemon on top.

### Steamed tilapia with ginger and soy (Wed dinner) · ~450 kcal plate · 51 g protein
- 170 g tilapia fillet
- 1 thumb ginger (thin strips), 2 green onions
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 cup bok choy, 1 cup brown rice

1. Put the fish on a heatproof plate, top with ginger. Steam 8 to 10 minutes until it flakes.
2. Pour soy and sesame oil over it, top with green onion.
3. Steam bok choy for the last 3 minutes. Serve with rice.

### Grilled pompano with tomato and onion salad (Thu, Sun dinner) · ~560 kcal plate · 41 g protein
- 1 pompano (about 150 g edible), cleaned
- Salt, pepper, calamansi
- Salad: 1 tomato, ¼ red onion, 1 tbsp vinegar, pinch of salt
- 1 cup brown rice

1. Score the fish, rub with salt, pepper and calamansi.
2. Grill, air-fry (200 °C / 400 °F, 15 to 18 min, flip halfway) or bake.
3. Chop tomato and onion, mix with vinegar and salt. Serve with rice.

### Sinigang na hipon (Wed lunch) · ~425 kcal with rice · 44 g protein (makes 2 servings; save one)
- 300 g shrimp, peeled
- 1 small radish (labanos), sliced; 6 okra; 1 tomato; ½ onion
- 2 cups kangkong or spinach; 1 long green chili (optional)
- ½ pack tamarind sinigang mix (or fresh tamarind), 5 cups water
- 1 cup brown rice per serving

1. Boil water with onion and tomato 5 minutes.
2. Add radish and okra, simmer 5 minutes. Stir in sinigang mix.
3. Add shrimp and chili, cook 2 to 3 minutes until pink. Add kangkong, turn off heat.
4. Use half the mix packet to keep the salt down. Taste before adding fish sauce.

## Chicken

### Light chicken adobo (Tue lunch) · ~530 kcal plate · 53 g protein (makes 2 servings)
- 300 g skinless chicken breast, cut in chunks
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 3 tbsp vinegar, ½ cup water
- 4 cloves garlic (smashed), 2 bay leaves, ½ tsp peppercorns, 1 tsp oil
- Per serving: 1 cup brown rice, 1 cup green beans

1. Brown garlic in the oil. Add chicken and sear 2 minutes.
2. Add soy, vinegar, water, bay leaves and pepper. Don't stir for 3 minutes (lets the vinegar cook off).
3. Simmer covered 15 minutes, then uncovered until the sauce thickens a little.
4. Steam or boil green beans 4 minutes. Serve with rice.

### Chicken pinakbet (Tue dinner) · ~525 kcal plate · 47 g protein (makes 2 servings)
- 240 g chicken breast, thin strips
- 1 small eggplant, 1 cup squash (kalabasa), 1 small ampalaya, 6 okra, 1 cup sitaw (long beans), all cut bite-size
- 1 tomato, ½ onion, 3 cloves garlic, 2 tsp oil
- 1 tbsp bagoong (light) or 1 tsp fish sauce, ½ cup water
- Per serving: 1 cup brown rice

1. Sauté garlic, onion and tomato in oil. Add chicken, cook 3 minutes.
2. Add bagoong and water, then squash. Cover 5 minutes.
3. Add the rest of the vegetables. Cover and cook 5 to 7 minutes until tender. Don't over-stir.

### Chicken tinola (Thu lunch) · ~400 kcal with ½ cup rice · 51 g protein (makes 2 servings)
- 300 g skinless chicken breast, chunks
- 1 thumb ginger (sliced), 3 cloves garlic, ½ onion, 1 tsp oil
- 1 sayote or green papaya, wedges
- 1 cup malunggay or spinach leaves
- 1 tbsp fish sauce, 4 cups water
- Per serving: ½ cup brown rice

1. Sauté ginger, garlic and onion in oil. Add chicken, cook 3 minutes. Add fish sauce.
2. Add water, simmer 15 minutes.
3. Add sayote, cook 5 to 7 minutes. Add malunggay, turn off heat.

### Pesto chicken with big salad (Fri lunch) · ~450 kcal · 48 g protein
- 150 g chicken breast
- 1 tbsp basil pesto, pinch of salt
- Optional: 15 g parmesan crisp (spoon grated parmesan into a circle on parchment, bake 6 minutes at 200 °C / 400 °F)
- Salad: 2 cups greens, cucumber, tomato, 1 tbsp vinaigrette

1. Spread pesto on the chicken. Bake at 200 °C / 400 °F for 18 to 22 minutes (or air-fry 15 minutes).
2. Slice and serve on the salad.

### Chicken tacos (Fri dinner) · ~380 kcal · 49 g protein
- 150 g chicken breast
- ½ tsp each: chili powder, cumin, garlic powder; salt; lime
- 2 corn tortillas
- Salsa, chopped onion, cilantro

1. Season chicken, cook in a sprayed pan 5 to 6 minutes per side. Rest, then slice.
2. Warm tortillas in a dry pan. Fill with chicken, salsa, onion and cilantro. Squeeze lime.

## Other

### Tuna wrap (Sat lunch) · ~340 kcal (+ apple 95) · 36 g protein
- 1 can (120 g drained) tuna in water
- 1 tbsp light mayo, 1 tsp mustard, chopped celery or onion, pepper
- 1 whole wheat tortilla, lettuce, tomato

### Lean beef sinigang (Sat dinner) · ~420 kcal with ½ cup rice · 38 g protein (makes 2 servings)
- 240 g lean sirloin, thin slices, fat trimmed
- 1 radish, 6 okra, 1 cup sitaw, 2 cups kangkong, 1 tomato, ½ onion
- ½ pack sinigang mix, 5 cups water
- Per serving: ½ cup brown rice

1. Boil water with onion and tomato. Add beef, simmer 20 to 30 minutes until tender; skim the foam.
2. Add radish and sitaw, 5 minutes. Add okra and sinigang mix, 3 minutes. Add kangkong, turn off heat.

## Extra recipes

### Light creamy spinach and sun-dried tomato pasta with chicken · ~330 kcal · 37 g protein (serves 4)
Lighter version of a Facebook "anti-inflammatory" recipe (original ~625 kcal and 14 g protein per serving, with 1 cup heavy cream).
- 4 oz (113 g) dry protein or whole wheat spaghetti (Barilla Protein+ or chickpea pasta)
- 450 g raw skinless chicken breast (or peeled shrimp), bite-size
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- 4 cups baby spinach
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt + ¼ cup pasta water
- ¼ cup grated parmesan
- ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper, pinch of red pepper flakes

1. Cook the pasta; save ½ cup of the water, then drain.
2. Heat the oil, cook the chicken 5 to 6 minutes until done. Add garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 1 minute.
3. Add spinach and stir until wilted. Turn off the heat.
4. Stir in the pasta, then the yogurt mixed with ¼ cup pasta water, and the parmesan. Off the heat so the yogurt does not split. Season.

### Lemon garlic tilapia (oven, air fryer or pan) · ~210 kcal · 34 g protein (1 fillet)
- 1 tilapia fillet, about 170 g raw
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
- ½ lemon or 2 calamansi, juiced
- ¼ tsp paprika, salt, pepper
- Parsley or green onion (optional)

1. Pat the fish dry. Rub with oil, garlic, paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Cook: oven 400 °F 12 to 15 min, or air fryer 400 °F 8 to 10 min (no flip), or nonstick pan 3 to 4 min per side.
3. Done when it flakes easily with a fork. Squeeze lemon on top, add herbs.
4. Serve with steamed broccoli or bok choy (~30 kcal) and ½ cup brown rice (~110 kcal) or none. Plate ~350 kcal.

## Vegetable sides (each makes ~2 servings)

Tips: fill half the plate with vegetables; use 1 tsp oil (1 tbsp = 120 kcal); go light on soy sauce and bagoong (salt).

### Garlic sautéed sitaw or green beans · ~90 kcal per serving
- 250 g sitaw or green beans, cut
- 1 tsp oil, 3 cloves garlic, ½ onion
- 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp water, pepper

1. Sauté garlic and onion in the oil.
2. Add beans and water, cover, cook 5 to 6 min until bright green and tender.
3. Season with soy sauce and pepper.

### Air-fryer broccoli or cauliflower · ~80 kcal
- 300 g broccoli or cauliflower florets
- 1 tsp olive oil, garlic powder, salt, pepper, lemon

1. Toss florets with oil and seasonings.
2. Air-fry 400 °F for 8 to 10 min, shake halfway.
3. Squeeze lemon on top.

### Bok choy with garlic · ~60 kcal
- 2 to 3 baby bok choy, halved
- 1 tsp oil, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tsp oyster sauce, 2 tbsp water

1. Brown garlic in the oil.
2. Add bok choy and water, cover 2 to 3 min.
3. Stir in oyster sauce.

### Ensaladang pipino at kamatis (cucumber and tomato salad) · ~40 kcal
- 1 cucumber, 2 tomatoes, ¼ red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp vinegar, pinch of salt, pepper (sweetener optional)

1. Mix everything, chill 10 min. Good with fish or grilled meat.

### Ensaladang talong (grilled eggplant salad) · ~70 kcal
- 2 Asian eggplants
- 1 tomato, ¼ onion, 2 tbsp vinegar, salt, pepper

1. Grill or air-fry the eggplants until soft (400 °F, about 15 min).
2. Peel, chop and mix with tomato, onion and vinegar.

### Light pinakbet · ~120 kcal
- 1 cup squash, 6 okra, 1 eggplant, ½ ampalaya, 1 cup sitaw, 1 tomato, ½ onion, 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp oil, 1 tsp bagoong (keeps salt down), ½ cup water

1. Sauté garlic, onion and tomato; stir in bagoong.
2. Add squash and water, simmer 5 min.
3. Add the rest, cover, cook 5 to 7 min. Don't overstir.
